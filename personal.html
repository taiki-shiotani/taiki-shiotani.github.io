About

I am a Research Associate (Assistant Professor) in the Yamaura Group at the Institute for Solid State Physics (ISSP), The University of Tokyo. My research focuses on magnetic and electronic properties of quantum materials, with particular interests in frustrated lattices, intermetallic magnets, crystal growth, and structure–property relationships.
