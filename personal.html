About

I am a Research Associate (Assistant Professor) in the Yamaura Group at the Institute for Solid State Physics (ISSP), The University of Tokyo. My research focuses on the discovery of new quantum materials, crystal growth, and magnetic and electronic properties. As a member of the X-ray Diffraction Laboratory, I also pursue crystallography-based condensed-matter research and support shared use of X-ray diffractometers.

Special skills

  • Single-crystal growth: arc melting, flux method, chemical vapor transport (CVT), etc.
  • Air-free and high-temperature synthesis: sealed-tube methods and air-free crystal growth above 1400 °C
  • Diffraction measurements: powder and single-crystal X-ray diffraction and neutron diffraction
  • Structure analysis: powder and single-crystal structure analysis and magnetic structure analysis
  • Elemental analysis: SEM–EDX and WDX
  • Physical-property measurements: magnetic, transport, and low-temperature measurements using MPMS and PPMS
  • Spectroscopy: nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) and electron spin resonance (ESR)
